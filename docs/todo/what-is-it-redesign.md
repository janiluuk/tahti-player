# What is Tahti page redesign

**Status:** open

## Goal

Rebuild `/what-is-it` so it is visual, engaging and short — reconstructed from
`../tahti-org/README.md` (+ product overview): who it is for, what it does,
and which features matter.

## Scope

- Replace the long pitch / outdated roadmap wall with three clear sections.
- Match For Artists visual language (`helpUi` tones, gradient hero, `ButtonLink`).
- EN + FI via content locale toggle.
- Storybook story; update public-route e2e heading if the title changes.

## Out of scope

- Marketing `website/` in tahti-org.
- Full rewrite of `/about` or `/how-it-works` (link to them instead).
