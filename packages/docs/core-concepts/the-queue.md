# The queue

The queue is what plays next. It sits in the panel on the right of the window; the queue button in the player bar shows or hides it.

- **Play** on a track starts it now. **Add to queue** puts it at the end.
- Drag a track to move it. With the keyboard, the arrow keys move between rows and **Delete** removes the focused one.
- **Clear queue** empties it. The **More queue actions** menu (the **…** button) saves the queue as a playlist (**Save queue as playlist**). In the desktop app the menu offers **Save queue to cloud playlist** and **Save queue as local playlist**, for the files on your computer.
- Long queues stay fast: only the rows on screen are drawn.

Shuffle and repeat are in the player bar.
