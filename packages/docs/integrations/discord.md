# Discord Rich Presence

The Tahti Player desktop app can show the track you're playing under "Listening to" on your Discord profile, with its artist, cover and time left.

## Turn it on

1. Make sure the Discord desktop app is running on the same computer.
2. Open Tahti Player, then go to Settings, then Integrations.
3. Turn **Discord Rich Presence** on.

It stays on the next time you start Tahti Player. If Discord wasn't running, the switch says it couldn't connect; start Discord and turn the switch on again.

## What Discord shows

- The track title and artist.
- The album, when the track has one.
- The cover, when it's an image on the web. Covers of files in your desktop library aren't shown, because Discord can't fetch them.
- Elapsed and remaining time while it plays. Paused tracks show no time, and radio and live streams show no time left.

Nothing is shown when nothing is playing.
