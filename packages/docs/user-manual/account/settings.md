# Settings

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone: **More → Settings**). Sections you can't use are hidden; for example, Artist, Channel & chat and Broadcast only show once you have a channel.

| Section | Tabs | What's there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Account** | Sign-in & security (Session, Password & email, Devices, Two-factor authentication, API tokens), Membership & billing, Notifications (Notifications & visibility, Mentions), Privacy & data (Privacy, Storage) | Your password and email address, signed-in devices, two-factor sign-in, membership and fan subscriptions, invoices, which notifications you get in the app and by email, blocked accounts, storage use, data export and account deletion, personal API tokens for the CLI and AI tools. |
| **Artist** | Profile (Identity, Story), Links & press, Artist branding | Your public artist profile. See [Your channel and profile](../studio/your-channel.md). |
| **Channel & chat** | Channel Designer, Discovery, Username & domain, Chat, Moderators | Your channel page and its chat. |
| **Broadcast** | Radio, Green room, Multistream | Defaults for going live. See [Broadcasting](../studio/broadcasting.md). |
| **Playback** | — | Skip length for Shift + ←/→, your own internet radio stations, and other player preferences. |
| **Integrations** | — | ListenBrainz and Last.fm scrobbling. In the desktop app also Tahti Jam, the MCP and MPD servers, Discord, and app updates. |
| **Themes** | Browse, Editor, Import JSON | Pick a theme, light/dark/dynamic mode, or make your own. See [Themes](../../themes/themes.md). |
| **Add-ons** | Listener, Artist | Visualizers, import sources, widgets and more. See [Add-ons](add-ons.md). |
| **Logs** | — | Recent app activity, for troubleshooting. |
| **What's new** | — | Release notes. |

## Security

**Settings → Account → Sign-in & security**:

- **Password & email:** change your password (enter the current one first). To change your email address, enter the new one and press **Send confirmation link**: the change takes effect when you open the link sent to the new address, and the old address is told once it's done. While a change waits, the tab shows the new address and when the link expires, with **Cancel change**.
- **Devices:** every browser and app signed in to your account. Sign out one device, or **sign out everywhere** (including this one).
- **Two-factor authentication:** turn on sign-in codes from an authenticator app, and keep the backup codes somewhere safe.
- **Session:** your current sign-in, and signing out.
- **API tokens:** create a token (read-only or with write access, optionally expiring) for the [command line](../reference/command-line.md) or an AI tool. Revoke it to cut access at once.

The tabs say which changes are also confirmed by email.

## Notifications

**Settings → Account → Notifications** chooses what you're told about, in the app and by email: social activity, money moves and, for artists, **Studio work** (an upload that can't be processed, or a render, stems split, export, import or distribution that finishes or fails). See [Notifications and support](notifications-and-support.md).

## Restrictions

If the board has restricted your account (for example, blocked uploads or bookings), **Settings → Account** lists each active restriction at the top, with what it blocks. The pages it affects, such as Upload and the schedule, say so too.

## Your data

**Privacy & data** lists what a data export contains before you request it, shows accounts you've blocked, and lets you delete your account.
