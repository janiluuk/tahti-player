# Settings

Open **Settings** at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone: **More → Settings**). Sections you can't use are hidden; for example, Artist, Channel & chat and Broadcast only show once you have a channel.

| Section | Tabs | What's there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Account** | Sign-in & security, Membership & billing, Notifications, Privacy & data (also Session, Two-factor authentication, API tokens) | Email and password, two-factor sign-in, your session, membership and fan subscriptions, invoices, which notifications you get by email, blocked accounts, data export and account deletion, personal API tokens for the CLI and AI tools. |
| **Artist** | Profile (Identity, Story), Links & press, Artist branding | Your public artist profile. See [Your channel and profile](../studio/your-channel.md). |
| **Channel & chat** | Channel Designer, Discovery, Username & domain, Chat, Moderators | Your channel page and its chat. |
| **Broadcast** | Radio, Green room, Multistream | Defaults for going live. See [Broadcasting](../studio/broadcasting.md). |
| **Playback** | — | Skip length for Shift + ←/→, your own internet radio stations, and other player preferences. |
| **Integrations** | — | ListenBrainz and Last.fm scrobbling. In the desktop app also Tahti Jam, the MCP and MPD servers, Discord, and app updates. |
| **Themes** | Browse, Editor, Import JSON | Pick a theme, light/dark/dynamic mode, or make your own. See [Themes](../../themes/themes.md). |
| **Add-ons** | Listener, Artist | Visualizers, import sources, widgets and more. See [Add-ons](add-ons.md). |
| **Logs** | — | Recent app activity, for troubleshooting. |
| **What's new** | — | Release notes. |

## Security

- **Two-factor authentication:** turn on sign-in codes from an authenticator app.
- **Session:** your current sign-in, and signing out.
- **API tokens:** create a token (read-only or with write access, optionally expiring) for the [command line](../reference/command-line.md) or an AI tool. Revoke it to cut access at once.

## Your data

**Privacy & data** lists what a data export contains before you request it, shows accounts you've blocked, and lets you delete your account.
