# Membership and governance

Tahti is run by Tahti ry, a nonprofit association: one member, one vote. Membership is €40 a year and supports the association; artist members also get unlimited live broadcasting and lossless listening on their channel.

Take or renew membership in **Settings → Account → Membership & billing** (payment through Stripe).

## Governance (members)

Open **Governance** (`/governance`; artists also see it as **Studio → Governance**):

- **Motions:** read proposals, discuss them, second a draft, and vote while a motion is open. A motion's page shows who seconded it, the result certificate when it closes, and the meeting it was taken up at.
- **Meetings:** agendas, documents and decisions of meetings and the AGM.
- **Members:** the member register.
- **History:** decided motions and the decision log.
- **Discussions** (`/governance/discussions`): topics the board opens for members to talk through. Reply to join in; replying follows the topic so new replies notify you, and **Mute notifications** keeps following quietly. You can remove your own reply while the topic is open. A discussion isn't a vote; decisions go through motions. The board opens, edits, pins and closes topics in **Admin → Governance → Discussions**.
- **Feature requests:** suggest and vote on improvements to Tahti.
- **Board:** the current board, board elections (stand for election or withdraw while nominations are open), annual reports, and the association's registered details and auditors.

Something wrong in your member details or in a published record? **Request a correction** on Governance; the board's answer appears there, and you can withdraw the request while it waits.

## Transparency (everyone)

`/transparency` publishes the year's money: income, grants paid to artists (with the methodology), and the board's resolutions. No account needed.
