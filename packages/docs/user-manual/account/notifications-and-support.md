# Notifications and support

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar collects what matters to your account: likes and comments on your content, new followers, mentions, finished processing, new posts from artists you follow, and messages from the board. Choose which of them also come by email in **Settings → Account → Notifications**.

The blue light at the top left is separate: it lists work still in progress, such as uploads and imports being processed.

## Platform status and news

- `/status` shows whether Tahti's services are up.
- `/news` has announcements from the Tahti team (the latest are on the front page).
- **Settings → What's new** lists app release notes.

## Contact support

Open **Help center → Contact support** (`/help/support`) for account, billing or technical issues. Signed in, the form uses your account email and lists your earlier requests with the board's replies, where you can answer.

The **Help center** in the app has short guides for most tasks, and **H** on any page starts a guided tour of it.
