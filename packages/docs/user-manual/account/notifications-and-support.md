# Notifications and support

## Notifications

The bell in the top bar collects what matters to your account: likes and comments on your content, new followers, mentions, finished or failed processing, new posts from artists you follow, and messages from the board. Choose what you're told about, in the app and by email, in **Settings → Account → Notifications**.

The bell's **See all** (or **Notifications** in the account menu on a phone) opens the **Notifications** page (`/notifications`):

- Filter by kind: **All**, **Social**, **Music**, **Money**, **Governance** or **Other**. Each chip shows how many unread notifications it has, and each notification is labelled with its kind.
- **Mark read** or **Remove** a single notification, **Mark all read**, or **Clear read** to remove everything you've already read.
- Notifications about Studio work that failed stand out, so a failed upload or export isn't missed.

The blue light at the top left is separate: it lists work still in progress, such as uploads and imports being processed.

## Platform status and news

- `/status` shows whether Tahti's services are up.
- `/news` has announcements from the Tahti team (the latest are on the front page).
- **Settings → What's new** lists app release notes.

## Contact support

Open **Help center → Contact support** (`/help/support`) for account, billing or technical issues. Signed in, the form uses your account email and lists your earlier requests with the board's replies, where you can answer. **Mark resolved** closes a request once you're sorted; replying to a resolved request opens it again.

The **Help center** in the app has short guides for most tasks, and **H** on any page starts a guided tour of it.
