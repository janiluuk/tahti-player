# Broadcasting

Everything for going live is under **Studio → Broadcast**. Its tabs: **Prep**, **Credentials**, **Recording**, **Destinations**, **Green room**, **Schedule**, **Events**, **Shows**, **Channel** and **Radio**.

## Before you start (Prep)

The checklist shows what's ready: show title, stream credentials and the encoder signal. **Confirm show info** sets what listeners see: show name, tagline, type, visibility and whether it's an episode of a series.

## Connect your software (Credentials)

- **OBS, Streamlabs, Larix (RTMP):** copy **Server** and **Stream key**. In OBS: Settings → Stream → Service *Custom*, paste both. Use 128–192 kbps AAC audio. **Ready-made OBS setup** downloads a scene preset with your server and key filled in.
- **Traktor, Mixxx and other Icecast apps:** choose your app under **Connect broadcasting software** to see the Server, Mount and Password to paste in.
- You can replace your stream key or Icecast password here if it leaks.

Start streaming in your app; your channel shows **Live** when the signal arrives.

## Mirror to other platforms (Destinations)

Stream once to Tahti and let Tahti forward it to YouTube, Twitch, Facebook, Kick, Mixcloud and other RTMP destinations. Add their keys here; don't also point your software at them directly. Defaults live in **Settings → Broadcast → Multistream**.

## Guests (Green room)

Open the green room, invite guests, see who's joined and remove anyone.

## Recordings

With recording on, finished broadcasts appear under **Library → Recordings**, where you can publish them as tracks. Defaults for recording and publishing are in **Settings → Broadcast**.

## Limits

Free artists get about one hour of live time a week; Tahti ry members have no limit. The **Prep** tab shows how much you have left.
