# Shows, schedule and events

## Shows and episodes

**Studio → Broadcast → Shows** holds your show series and their episodes. A recurring show appears in your channel schedule; each broadcast can be tied to an episode in **Prep → Confirm show info**.

## Schedule

**Studio → Broadcast → Schedule** is your programme: upcoming shows, your 24/7 rotation and bookings. Listeners see it on your channel.

## Tahti Radio slots

To broadcast on Tahti Radio itself, open **Radio** and **Book a slot** in the booking calendar. Booked shows appear in the Tahti Radio programme (`/schedule`).

## Events

**Studio → Broadcast → Events** lists your gigs and in-person shows: title, place, city, date and time, and a link. Upcoming events appear on your channel and in the **Coming up** row on the front page. **New event** adds one; edit or delete it from the list.
