# Shows, schedule and events

## Shows and episodes

**Studio → Broadcast → Shows** holds your show series and their episodes. A recurring show appears in your channel schedule; each broadcast can be tied to an episode in **Prep → Confirm show info**.

## Schedule

**Studio → Broadcast → Schedule** is your programme: upcoming shows, your 24/7 rotation and bookings. Listeners see it on your channel.

The **Channel programme** panel there holds the switches that shape what plays around your live shows: announcements in the programme, **Fallback / autoplay when offline**, and **Auto-enroll new archive into fallback**. (They used to live in Settings → Broadcast; Settings now links here.)

## Tahti Radio slots

To broadcast on Tahti Radio itself, open **Radio** and **Book a slot** in the booking calendar. Booked shows appear in the Tahti Radio programme (`/schedule`).

## Events

**Studio → Broadcast → Events** lists your gigs and in-person shows, with tabs for **Upcoming**, **Past** and **Invites**.

- **New event** takes a title, place, city, start and (optional) end time, an event page link, a ticket link and a cover image link. **Preview** shows the event the way listeners will see it. **Save as draft** keeps it private; **Publish** makes it public and tells your followers.
- On an event's page you can **Tag someone**: other Tahti artists, DJs or hosts playing it, each as performer, host or guest. A tag shows publicly once that person accepts it, and accepted events also appear on their channel.
- **Invites** lists events other artists tagged you on; **Accept** or **Decline**. A tag notification opens this tab directly.
- **Publish** can also announce the event on your connected Mastodon and Bluesky accounts (connect them in Settings → Artist). The event page then shows each announcement as Sending, Posted or Failed, with **Retry** on a failure.
- **Cancel event** keeps it listed with a **Cancelled** badge so people who planned to come see it; you can publish it again later.

Published events appear on your channel, in the **Coming up** row on the front page, and in the public calendar (**Discover → Feed → Calendar**), where each event has its own page with **Add to Google Calendar** and a subscribe (.ics) link.
