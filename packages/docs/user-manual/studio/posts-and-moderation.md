# Posts, newsletter and moderation

## Posts

**Studio → Posts** writes updates that reach your followers' **Feed**. Posts can be scheduled; followers are notified when one goes out. Delete a post from its menu.

## Newsletter

**Studio → Posts → Newsletter** composes and sends newsletters, with drafts and subscriber counts. Listeners subscribe from your profile; signed-out visitors confirm by email, and every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe.

## Moderate chat and comments

- Add moderators in **Settings → Channel & chat → Moderators**; they get a notification and a moderation page for your channel.
- Moderators remove messages and ban people from the chat, including straight from a message. The banned list is in the moderation page.
- **Settings → Channel & chat → Chat** decides whether chat is on and who may post (for example fan subscribers only).
- Turn comments off per track in its details, or for new uploads in **Settings → Account**.

Reports from listeners about your content go to the Tahti board.
