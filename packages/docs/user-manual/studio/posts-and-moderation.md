# Posts, newsletter and moderation

## Posts

**Studio → Fans → Posts** writes updates that show on your channel page and in your followers' feed (**Discover → Feed**). Posts can be scheduled; followers are notified when one goes out. Your posts show as story cards, the same as listeners see them; each card has buttons to preview, edit (the pencil) and delete it.

## Newsletter

**Studio → Fans → Newsletter** composes and sends newsletters, with drafts and subscriber counts. Listeners subscribe from your profile; signed-out visitors confirm by email, and every newsletter has a one-click unsubscribe. The trash icon (**Remove**) takes a draft or a past send off your list, after you confirm; a newsletter that is queued to go out can't be removed.

## Moderate chat and comments

Chat settings and moderation are on **Studio → Fans**, under **Chat** below the cards (**Settings → Channel & chat → Chat** and **Moderators** link there):

- **Enable live chat on my channel** turns chat on or off. **Show today's listener count in my chat** shows listeners how many people tuned in today.
- **Chat access** decides who may post (for example fan subscribers only).
- **Channel moderators** adds moderators; they get a notification and a moderation page for your channel. Moderators remove messages and ban people from the chat, including straight from a message.
- **Recent messages** lists the latest chat messages, and **Chat bans** lists who is banned.
- Turn comments off per track in its details, or for new uploads in **Settings → Account**.

Reports from listeners about your content go to the Tahti board.
