# Releases and distribution

## Releases

**Studio → Releases** is for albums, EPs and singles.

1. Press **New release**, give it a title and type, then add the tracklist, artwork, date and description.
2. Each track can have credits and several audio versions (upload a new version and switch which one is live).
3. **Publish** makes the release and its smart link visible.

A release's tabs include **Stats**, **Smart links** (the `/r/<slug>` page that lists where to listen, with click counts) and **Export**.

After you publish, the release page offers to **register it on MusicBrainz**, the open music database players and stores use. Choose **Always offer this** or **Don't ask again**; the prepared form opens on MusicBrainz for you to submit.

## Collections and playlists

**Library → Collections** holds albums, EPs, playlists, DJ sets, podcasts and show series.

- **New collection** (the **+**) creates one; pick its style.
- On its page, **+** (**Add content**) adds tracks from your library. Tracks still being processed are shown but can't be added until they're ready.
- Set its visibility (Public, Unlisted or Private), design its cover and text layer, and delete it from the **⋮** menu.

Public playlists can be subscribed to, embedded and played together in a [Jam](../listening/jam.md).

## Catalog IDs

**Release ops → Catalog & credits** stores the identifiers you already have. Turn on only the ones you use:

- **UPC / EAN:** the barcode for the whole release.
- **MusicBrainz** and **Discogs** IDs: links to those open catalogs. The guides prepare the title, barcode, credits and tracklist to copy across.
- **P-line / C-line / label imprint:** recording and artwork copyright and the label name.

If you don't know a value, leave it off; don't invent identifiers.

## Distribution

**Studio → Distribution** delivers eligible releases to streaming services through Revelator (fees may apply), shows delivery status, connects your Spotify artist profile and lists royalties.
