# Groups

A group is a collective of artists with a shared playlist, the **Collective playlist**. Members add their sounds to it; the group can ask admins to approve new sounds first.

## Find and join a group

**Discover → Groups** (or `/groups`) lists public groups, with **Search groups**. A group's page (`/groups/<slug>`) shows who started it, its members and their roles (**Owner**, **Admin** or **Member**), and whether anyone can join and how new sounds are added.

- **Join group** joins straight away. If admins approve new members, the button is **Request to join**, then **Request pending** until they answer.
- **Collective playlist** opens the group's playlist, and **Listen together** starts a [Jam](../listening/jam.md) on it.
- **Leave group** leaves it.

To add one of your sounds to a group, open the sound in **Library → Sounds**, then use the **Groups** card on its **Playlists** tab (or in the track's edit dialog). If the group's admins approve sounds first, it waits for their answer.

## Start a group

Open **Studio → Music → Groups** and press **New group**:

1. Give it a **Name** and a **Description**.
2. **Who can join:** **Anyone can join**, or **Request to join** (admins approve new members).
3. **New sounds:** **Members add right away**, or **Admins approve first**.
4. **Public** groups appear in the groups directory.
5. Press **Create group**. You become the owner, and the collective playlist is created for you.

**Your groups** lists every group you're in. A badge shows when sounds or requests wait for review.

## Manage a group

Open a group under **Studio → Music → Groups** (or **Manage** on its public page). Owners and admins see:

- **Settings:** name, description, cover image, who can join, how new sounds are added, and whether it's public. **Save settings** keeps the changes. Only the owner can **Delete group**, which removes the group and its playlist for everyone.
- **Members:** everyone in the group with their role.
  - Owners and admins can **Remove** members. Only the owner can remove an admin, and nobody can remove the owner. A removed member is told, and the tracks they added stay in the playlist.
  - The owner can **Make admin** (or **Make member** to undo it) and **Hand over** the group. Handing over makes that person the owner, with the playlist; you stay in the group as an admin, and only they can hand it back.
- **Join requests:** **Approve** or **Decline** people asking to join.
- **Sound submissions:** **Approve** or **Reject** sounds waiting to join the playlist.

Group notices, such as a join request or a role change, arrive in your **Notifications** under **Social**.
