# Studio: getting started

## Become an artist

Listeners see **Become an artist** in the sidebar. Joining as an artist gives you a channel (`/channel/<slug>`) and an artist profile (`/u/<username>`).

| | Free artist | Tahti ry member (€40/year) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Live broadcasting | About 1 hour a week (resets Monday 00:00 UTC) | Unlimited |
| Listeners hear | MP3 ~192 kbps; your archive plays while you're offline | Lossless on member channels |
| Governance | — | Vote, propose motions, stand for the board |

Membership supports the association that runs Tahti; it isn't a consumer plan. See [Membership and governance](../account/membership-and-governance.md).

To create your channel, open **Studio** and follow the channel setup: pick your slug (the `/channel/…` address) and name.

## Find your way around

**Studio** in the sidebar has these sections (tabs at the top):

| Tab | What it's for |
| --- | --- |
| **Home** | Your Studio home. Small shortcuts come first (Go live, Schedule, Music, Upload, Collections, Releases, Updates, Stats, Revenue, Channel look…), then recent activity, recent broadcasts, upcoming shows and governance. |
| **Music** | **Releases**, **Distribution** to streaming services, the audio **Editor**, and **Groups** (collectives with a shared playlist). See [Releases and distribution](releases-and-distribution.md) and [Groups](groups.md). |
| **Broadcast** | A page that shows whether you are on air with a **Go live** button, the **Channel manager** (live status, rotation and stream controls), everything **Coming up** (shows, events and listening parties, with All / Shows / Events / Parties chips), and cards for Schedule, Shows, Events, Listening parties, 24/7 radio, Recordings, Tahti Radio and Channel. Broadcast's pages (**Go live**, **Schedule**, **Shows**, **Channel**) also sit in a row of links under the Studio tabs; the **Broadcast** tab takes you back to this page. Events and listening parties are filters on **Schedule**, and 24/7 radio is the **Radio** section of **Channel**. See [Broadcasting](broadcasting.md). |
| **Fans** | A page of cards: **Posts**, **Newsletter**, **Memberships** (fan tiers), **Store** (items for sale and track sales) and **Earnings** (orders, payouts, grants, Stripe). Each opens its own page with a **← Fans** link back. Below the cards, **Chat** turns your channel chat on or off and holds its moderation. See [Posts and moderation](posts-and-moderation.md) and [Stats and earnings](stats-and-earnings.md). |
| **Stats** | Plays, downloads, followers and listening for a period you choose, plus listeners and top lists, on one page. |
| **Profile** | How you look to listeners: cards for each part of your artist profile (identity, story, people, links, branding, gallery, press kit, release visuals) and your channel page (design, how fans find you, address and domain, chat, moderators). |

Members find motions, discussions, documents and the governance guide under **Co-op** in the sidebar (no longer a Studio tab).

Your music files live under **Library** (sidebar): **Sounds**, **Collections**, **Upload**, **Recordings**, **Media**, **Stash**, **Embeds**, **Smart links** and **Local files**. See [Uploads and tracks](uploads-and-tracks.md).

Your public look, profile and press kit are in **Settings → Artist** and **Settings → Channel & chat**. See [Your channel](your-channel.md).

## View first, then edit

Tracks, releases, collections, shows and episodes in Studio open read-only. Press the pencil (**Edit**, or for example **Edit details**) to show the form. This adds `?edit=true` to the address, so a bookmarked or shared link opens straight into editing. While you edit, the pencil becomes a check (**Done editing**). Events open read-only too, and their pencil opens the event's edit page.
