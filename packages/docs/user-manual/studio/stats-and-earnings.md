# Stats and earnings

## Stats

**Studio → Stats** has **Plays & listeners** (with a range picker and a detail view) and **Top lists**. Each track's **Insights** tab shows its own plays and listeners, and the listener map shows where people listen from. Artists see anonymous counts, never who listened.

The Studio home shows plays today, total plays and downloads, and followers. The Stats overview also shows how download gates (follow or share to download) convert.

## Fan tiers

**Studio → Audience → Tiers** creates monthly tiers with a price and perks, and turns them on or off. Once a tier is active, **Subscribe** appears on your channel and profile.

**Studio → Audience** also lists the latest orders, subscriber and payout statistics, and how a typical order splits. **Stripe** connects your payout account (Stripe Connect) and opens its dashboard.

## Sell tracks

Purchase tiers let listeners buy single tracks: set the price tiers, then put a track on sale (or limit it to buyers) in its edit dialog. The track page then shows **Buy this track**.

## How a payment splits

On a €5 fan-subscription order, Stripe keeps about €0.45 (2.9% + €0.30), Tahti keeps €0.10 (a 2% operational fee), and you receive about €4.45 through Stripe Connect. Your Stripe dashboard shows what actually reached your bank.

When a fan cancels they keep their perks until the end of the period, plus about seven days of grace; nothing to do on your side.

## Grants

Downloads and fan-support euros count toward yearly engagement. Eligible artists share the cooperative's grant pool on top of what each order already paid out. The year's figures are public on `/transparency`.
