# Your channel and profile

## Channel Designer

Open **Settings → Channel & chat → Channel Designer**, or **Edit design** on your channel page. The preview shows what listeners see.

- Pick a block (releases, tracks, latest, feed, news, player, backdrop) and hide it with the eye button.
- **Player** sets the stage, visualizer, gradient and now-playing overlay.
- **Backdrop** sets the header style, a video, image or slideshow backdrop, and page colors.
- Presets give you a starting look. **Save layout** when you're done.

Other **Channel & chat** tabs: **Discovery** (show or hide the share button), **Username & domain** (rename your channel, or use your own domain), **Chat** (who can chat) and **Moderators**.

## Artist profile

**Settings → Artist**:

- **Profile:** **Identity** (display name, nameplate, what you do) and **Story** (your bio).
- **Links & press:** social links and your press kit.
- **Artist branding:** profile picture, logo (on avatar, cover or both) and the **Gallery** of photos on your profile. Use the **+** in the gallery header to add photos, drag to reorder, and turn **Public** on to show it.

## Widgets on your channel

Install channel widgets from **Settings → Add-ons → Channel**; they show on your channel and profile. Anyone can build one; see [Disco-widgets](../account/add-ons.md).

## Your channel's 24/7 radio

**Studio → Broadcast → Radio** (and **Channel**) choose what your channel plays when you're not live: your rotation, playback settings and the Tahti Radio rotation you take part in. New uploads can join the rotation automatically.
