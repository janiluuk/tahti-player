# Your local music (desktop app)

The desktop app keeps a library of the music files on your computer, alongside Tahti.

## Add your music

Open **Library → Local files** and add a folder. The app imports MP3, FLAC, WAV, AIFF, M4A, OGG and more, and keeps watching the folder for changes. Local tracks play in the same player and queue as Tahti tracks.

Browse by **Tracks**, **Artists**, **Albums**, **Genres** and **Folders**, with counts, total length and size. Edit tags, and analyse BPM, key and loudness.

## Playlists of your own files

**Library → Local files** also keeps local playlists: create, rename, duplicate and delete them, reorder tracks (drag, or Alt + ↑/↓), undo edits, import and export M3U/M3U8, and play or shuffle them. **Smart playlists** fill themselves from rules (for example "added in the last 30 days").

From the queue, **Save queue as local playlist** keeps your local tracks; **Save queue to cloud playlist** saves Tahti tracks to your account.

## In the browser

The web app also has **Library → Local files**, using the browser's file picker. Those files stay on your device for that browser session; reloading clears the list. To put a track on Tahti, use **Studio → Upload**.
