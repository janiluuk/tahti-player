# Troubleshooting

## Playback

- **A radio station won't play.** Some stations block playback from other websites or are temporarily off air; the player says so. Try again later, or use the station's own site.
- **Nothing happens when I press Space.** Space plays and pauses only when a track is loaded and no text field or button has focus. Click an empty part of the page first.
- **A track shows "Processing".** It was just uploaded or edited and can't be played yet. It becomes playable on its own when processing finishes.

## Signing in

- **"Invalid email or password."** Check the address you joined with, or use **Forgot password?** on the sign-in form.
- **"Too many requests."** Several sign-in attempts in a short time are paused for a minute. Wait and try again.
- **New account can't sign in.** Confirm your email address first: open the link in the verification email (check spam).

## Uploads and imports

- **An upload failed.** The message names the problem (for example an unsupported file type). Supported: FLAC, WAV, AIFF, MP3, M4A, OGG, up to 4 GB.
- **A track can't be added to a release.** It's still processing; add it once it's ready.
- **An import didn't appear.** Check **Import progress** on the Upload page: it shows queued, running, finished and failed imports with the reason.

## Broadcasting

- **My channel doesn't show Live.** Check the **Prep** checklist in Studio → Broadcast: the encoder signal turns green when your software is connected. Make sure the server and stream key are pasted exactly and that your software is streaming.
- **I ran out of live time.** Free artists have about an hour a week (resets Monday 00:00 UTC); Tahti ry members have no limit.

## Still stuck?

Open **Help center → Contact support**. **Settings → Logs** shows recent app activity you can include in your message.
