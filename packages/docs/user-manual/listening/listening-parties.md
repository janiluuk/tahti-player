# Listening parties

A listening party is a scheduled première: at the start time, the artist's release plays once, start to finish, and everyone at the party hears the same track at the same moment. The artist joins the chat to tell the story behind the music.

## Join a party

Parties have their own page at `/party/<id>`. You reach it from the artist's link, or from the banner on their channel page while a party is coming up or live.

- **Before the start** the page counts down to showtime. Choose **Join at the start** and leave the page open: playback begins on its own when the party starts.
- **During the party** choose **Join the party**. Playback starts in the player at the point everyone else has reached, so joining late puts you in sync, not at track one. If your player drifts more than a couple of seconds, the page nudges it back.
- **Leave** stops following the party. Playing something else also leaves it.

The page shows:

- the tracklist, with the playing track highlighted and how far into it the party is,
- the liner notes and credits for the release,
- the artist's chat, the same chat as on their channel,
- the artist's purchase options, when their store is open.

Tracks of a release that is not out yet play only during the party, and only up to the track the party has reached.

When the party is over, the page says so and links to the release once it is published. A canceled party says it was canceled.

## Host a party (artists)

1. In Studio, open a release under **Music → Releases** and choose **Schedule listening party** (the party icon in the release header). Every track needs its audio uploaded first.
2. Pick the start time (at least five minutes from now, and within a year) and, if you like, a note to listeners. The note shows on the party page.
3. Share the party link. Your channel page also shows a banner for it.

Your parties are listed under **Studio → Broadcast → Listening parties**. Until a party starts you can **Reschedule** it (new time or note) or **Cancel** it. Two parties on your channel cannot overlap.

During the party, chat as you normally would; the chat posts a "Now playing" line as each track starts. Fans buy through your purchase tiers, which the party page shows while your store is open and Stripe is connected; set them up under **Studio → Audience**.

{% hint style="info" %}
The release plays in each listener's own player; there is no stream to start or stop. Formats such as vinyl, and pre-orders, are not part of listening parties yet.
{% endhint %}
