# The front page (Listen)

**Listen** is the first item in the sidebar and the page you land on. You don't need an account to use it.

## What's on it

From the top:

| Section | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| **Welcome** | Signed-out visitors only: what Tahti is, with **Join free**, **Tahti for artists** and **What is Tahti?**. The **×** hides it for good on that browser. |
| **Recently played** and **Tahti Selects** | Side by side: what you played last, to pick up where you left off, and tracks picked by the Tahti Selects curators. Until you have played something, only Tahti Selects shows. |
| **New releases** | The newest albums, EPs and singles from Tahti artists. A card opens the release's page with all the places to listen. |
| **Live now** | Channels broadcasting right now. When nobody is live it says so. |
| **Replays** | Channels replaying a recent broadcast. Only shown when there are some. |
| **Trending this week** | The week's most played tracks, ranked. |
| **Coming up** | Upcoming shows and gigs from Tahti artists, soonest first. Hidden when nothing is scheduled. |
| **New tracks** | The latest uploads, as a track list. |
| **Radio** | Internet radio stations (Finnish and international). See [Radio](radio.md). |
| **Widgets** | Disco-widgets you installed from Settings → Add-ons. |

New visitors, and listeners who haven't played anything yet, see the **Radio** row first; once you've played something, Recently played leads again. Tahti Radio itself is on the [Radio](radio.md) page, and the news from the Tahti team is on **Discover** (see [Discover and search](discover-and-search.md)).

Rows have arrows to scroll them, and most have a filter box. The list icon in a row's toolbar shows it as a compact list instead of cards (**Show as list** / **Show as cards**); on a phone, rows start as lists. On a card, the play button starts that track or channel and the queue button lines it up next; the title opens its page.

If one section can't load, it says so and offers **Retry** without blocking the rest of the page.

## History

The **History** tab at the top of Listen is what you played recently, with listening stats. See [Listening history](history.md).

Posts from artists you follow are in **Discover → Feed**.

## Personalise it

Signed-in listeners can add widgets to Listen with the **+** button at the top right (Settings → Add-ons → Discovery), and choose which internet radio stations appear. A new widget goes to the top.

**Order sections** (the arrows icon in the Listen header) shows a drag handle on every section and widget; drag them (or move them with the keyboard) into the order you want. Your order is kept and wins over the defaults.

All your News add-on feeds show as one **Latest News** row, newest first, each story with its picture, its source and when it was published. The arrows page through the row, **more News** lists every story, and **Sources** adds or removes feeds: pick from the suggested DJ and music-production feeds or paste any RSS or Atom address. Articles open inside Tahti with their text and image, and **Read on** the source site if you want the original.
