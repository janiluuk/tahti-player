# The player

## Play something

Press play on a track, channel, release, playlist or radio station. The player bar appears at the bottom of the window once something is loaded; until then it stays hidden.

The player bar has play/pause, previous and next, shuffle and repeat (for tracks; live streams can't be shuffled), the seek bar, volume, and the [queue](the-queue.md) button. On radio, repeat becomes **Loop stations**: when a station ends, the next one in your list plays.

The seek bar sits under the controls, with the time played on its left and the time left on its right. Click anywhere on it to jump there, or drag its handle (it appears when you point at the bar) and let go where you want to be. The arrow keys move it while it has focus.

**Space** plays and pauses from anywhere (not while typing or while a button has focus). **Shift + ←/→** skips backward or forward. See [Keyboard shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## Full-screen player

Press the artwork or the track title in the player bar, or **V**, to open the full-screen player with a larger cover, the visualizer and the queue. On a phone, expand the compact player above the bottom navigation.

To stop a phone or tablet from dimming while the full-screen player plays, turn on **Settings → Playback → Keep the screen on in full screen**.

## Visualizers

Channels can show a visualizer chosen by the artist. Pick your own in **Settings → Add-ons → Visualizers**, or turn them off for a quieter screen. In the full-screen player, the visualizer menu shows a small animated preview of each mode.

Visualizer colours drift slowly between nearby tones while they play. With your system's reduce-motion setting on, they stay still.

## Tracks from hearthis.at, Mixcloud and other services

Some tracks on Tahti play from another service's own player (hearthis.at, Mixcloud, Spotify, Bandcamp or Mikseri.net). When you play one, that player appears at its own size just above the player bar, and you use its play and pause buttons; the player bar shows the track but no play/pause of its own, with **Plays in the … player above**. Next, previous, shuffle and repeat still move through your queue. Pressing play on the track again starts its player from the beginning.

## When something won't play

If a stream or track can't be played, a message says so (for example, a radio station that is off air or blocks playback) and the player bar shows **Can't connect — press play to retry**. Press play to try again.

Tracks still being processed after an upload show a **Processing** spinner and can't be played until processing finishes.

{% hint style="info" %}
The browser asks before you reload or close the tab while something is playing, so a stray click doesn't cut the music.
{% endhint %}

## On a phone

The lock screen and the system media controls can play, pause, skip, seek to a position, and stop. Stop pauses the player.

If a stream fails and the network comes back, the player tries that stream again on its own.

If the system pauses the audio while Tahti is in the background, the player shows **paused** when you come back.

The app keeps its edges, the bottom bar, and the side drawers clear of the screen's rounded corners and the home indicator.
