# The player

## Play something

Press play on a track, channel, release, playlist or radio station. The player bar appears at the bottom of the window once something is loaded; until then it stays hidden.

The player bar has play/pause, previous and next, shuffle and repeat (for tracks; live streams can't be shuffled), the seek bar, volume, and the [queue](the-queue.md) button.

**Space** plays and pauses from anywhere (not while typing or while a button has focus). **Shift + ←/→** skips backward or forward. See [Keyboard shortcuts](../reference/keyboard-shortcuts.md).

## Full-screen player

Press the artwork in the player bar, or **V**, to open the full-screen player with a larger cover, the visualizer and the queue. On a phone, expand the compact player above the bottom navigation.

## Visualizers

Channels can show a visualizer chosen by the artist. Pick your own in **Settings → Add-ons → Visualizers**, or turn them off for a quieter screen.

## When something won't play

If a stream or track can't be played, a message says so (for example, a radio station that is off air or blocks playback) and the player bar shows **Can't connect — press play to retry**. Press play to try again.

Tracks still being processed after an upload show a **Processing** spinner and can't be played until processing finishes.

{% hint style="info" %}
The browser asks before you reload or close the tab while something is playing, so a stray click doesn't cut the music.
{% endhint %}
