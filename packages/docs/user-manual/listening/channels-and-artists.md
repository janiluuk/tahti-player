# Channels and artists

Every artist has two public pages:

- **Channel** (`/channel/<slug>`): their live stage. When they're broadcasting you hear the show; when they're off air it plays their 24/7 rotation or archive.
- **Artist profile** (`/u/<username>`): their music, releases, playlists, photos, press kit and links.

A channel also opens at `<slug>.tahti.live`, as in the old app. `radio.tahti.live` opens Radio.

## On a channel page

The stage at the top shows what's playing, with its play button at the top left, beside the title. Under the stage, tabs split the page:

- **Music:** the sections the artist arranged for the channel, such as tracks, releases and upcoming shows, and their store.
- **Embeds:** tracks the artist hosts on other sites (hearthis.at, Mixcloud and similar). Only shown when the channel has some.
- **Comments:** the channel's comment wall.
- **Chat:** the live chat room. Hidden when the artist has turned chat off.

A track, release or playlist without a cover shows the default artwork. On a radio channel, the radio tower icon (**Open radio page**) opens [Radio](radio.md).

## Track rows

A channel's **Tracks**, an artist's **Popular tracks**, playlists and Listen's **New tracks** show each track as a row:

- The round **play** button, the artist and the title (which opens the track page).
- The **genre** and the **length** at the top right.
- The **waveform**. Click or drag it to jump within the track that's playing.
- Below it: **♡ with a count** to love the track (signed in), **add to queue**, **＋ add to a playlist** (signed in) and **⤓ download** when the artist allows downloads. If the artist asks you to follow or share first, the download opens the track page to do that.
- On the right: when the track came out, and how many likes, plays, downloads and comments it has.

The same rows appear on an artist's **Liked tracks**, on Favorites → **Liked tracks**, on playlists, on release pages and in a channel's **Playlist** widget. A track's own page shows its plays next to its comments, reposts, downloads and likes.

The buttons above the list switch between large rows, smaller rows, a grid and a plain list, and the sort icon (**Sort tracks**) opens a menu of orders, with the current one ticked. Both choices are remembered. Every list of tracks uses the same sort icon.

## On a track page

A track page has a compact header with the square cover, play, the counts and the action buttons. Only the track's owner sees the pencil (**Edit**).

The description and the **Comments** are in the main column. On the right, an icon tab bar holds:

- **Info**: the tracklist when the track has one (press a line to jump there) and the track's details.
- **Collections**: public playlists and collections the track is in.
- **Liked** and **Reposted**: the people who liked or reposted it.
- **Played by**: signed-in listeners and how many times each played it. Anonymous plays are never shown.

Point at an icon to see its name. **Show more** loads the rest of a tab.

## Follow an artist

Signed in, press **Follow** on the channel header (or the heart on the channel's player when they're on air). The button changes to **Following**. You'll see their new posts and releases in **Discover → Feed**, and they appear under **Favorites**.

Following is free. Supporting an artist with money is separate; see [Support an artist](supporting-artists.md).

## Share and embed

- **Share** on a channel, track, release or playlist copies its link. Unlisted items can be opened by anyone with the link but don't appear in discovery.
- **Embed** gives an iframe code to put a public track, release, playlist or channel on another website. The item has to stay public for the embed to work.
- A channel's share dialog also has **Copy RSS feed** for its podcast feed. If the artist has hidden Share, an RSS button copies the feed instead.

## Report something

The flag button on a channel, track, release, playlist or comment sends a report to the Tahti board. You don't need an account.

## Venues

**Discover → Venues** lists clubs and places that stream on Tahti. Run a venue? Register it there; **My venues** (`/venues/mine`) shows whether the board has verified it, and lets you edit it and book or cancel shows.
