# Channels and artists

Every artist has two public pages:

- **Channel** (`/channel/<slug>`): their live stage. When they're broadcasting you hear the show; when they're off air it plays their 24/7 rotation or archive, and lists their tracks, releases, schedule and comments.
- **Artist profile** (`/u/<username>`): their music, releases, playlists, photos, press kit and links.

## Follow an artist

Signed in, press **Follow** on the channel header (or the heart on the channel's player when they're on air). The button changes to **Following**. You'll see their new posts and releases in your **Feed**, and they appear under **Favorites**.

Following is free. Supporting an artist with money is separate; see [Support an artist](supporting-artists.md).

## Share and embed

- **Share** on a channel, track, release or playlist copies its link. Unlisted items can be opened by anyone with the link but don't appear in discovery.
- **Embed** gives an iframe code to put a public track, release, playlist or channel on another website. The item has to stay public for the embed to work.
- The **RSS** button copies the channel's podcast feed.

## Report something

The flag button on a channel, track, release, playlist or comment sends a report to the Tahti board. You don't need an account.

## Venues

**Discover → Venues** lists clubs and places that stream on Tahti. Run a venue? Register it there; **My venues** (`/venues/mine`) shows whether the board has verified it, and lets you edit it and book or cancel shows.
