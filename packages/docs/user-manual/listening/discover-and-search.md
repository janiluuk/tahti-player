# Discover and search

**Discover** (in the sidebar) has three tabs:

- **Discover:** a page of widgets you arrange yourself (**Add a widget**), such as "This week: most played". Filters narrow it to tracks you haven't heard, to a kind of content, or to the last 1 to 30 days.

  **Community stories** lead the page: the newest posts from every artist, as wide cards with a picture (the post's own, or the artist's newest track cover, or their avatar). Press a story's picture or title to read it in full: the whole post, its pictures and link, and the other community updates beside it, with **Newer** and **Older** to move through them. The author's name opens their profile.

  Track rows in widgets show the artist's avatar beside their name. Press a row to play it; hold **Shift** to add it to the queue instead. The **Random track** widget plays something new from the genres you pick (it remembers them).
- **Artists:** the artist directory, with search and filters.
- **Venues:** clubs and places that stream on Tahti.

## Search

The search box in the top bar finds artists, tracks and playlists as you type:

- Use the arrow keys to move through the suggestions and **Enter** to open one.
- Press **Enter** without choosing a suggestion to open the full results page (`/search?q=…`) with every matching artist, track and playlist or release.
- A tag on a track page opens all tracks with that tag (`/search?tag=…`).

Tracks in search results and on tag pages play right there, with the same track rows as elsewhere: play, add to queue, love and add to a playlist. A track only fan subscribers or buyers can hear shows a lock (**Members** or **Buy**) and opens its track page, which explains how to get it.
