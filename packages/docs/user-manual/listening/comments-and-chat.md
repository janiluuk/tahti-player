# Comments, chat and messages

## Comment on a moment

Open a track's page and pause or seek to the moment you want to talk about. Under the timeline:

- Pick an emoji to react at that timestamp.
- The comment icon opens a comment that starts with the current time, so others know where to listen.

You need to be signed in. Artists can turn comments off per track.

## Channel chat

Each channel has a live chat, on the channel page and at `/chat/<slug>`. Open **Chat** in the sidebar footer (on a phone: **More**) to pick a channel.

- Signed-out visitors may be asked to pass a captcha before joining a live chat.
- Some channels limit chat to fan subscribers.
- Moderators can remove messages and ban people from a channel's chat.

## Private messages

**Messages** (the speech bubble in the top bar) are direct messages between accounts. They're separate from channel chat. You can block an account from its profile or the message thread; blocking stops messages, comments, follows and mentions both ways (**Settings → Account → Privacy & data** lists who you've blocked).
