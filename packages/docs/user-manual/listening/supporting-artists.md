# Support an artist

## Fan subscriptions

Artists can offer monthly **fan tiers**. When an artist has at least one, their channel and profile show **Subscribe**; it opens `/subscribe/<username>` with the tiers, their price and perks. Payment goes through Stripe.

- Manage or cancel your subscriptions in **Settings → Account → Membership & billing**.
- After cancelling you keep the perks until the end of the billing period, plus about a week of grace.

## Buy a track

Some tracks are for sale. The track page shows **Buy this track**, and the artist's store section on their profile lists what's for sale. A track can also be limited to buyers or fan subscribers; its page then says what unlocks it.

## Downloads

If the artist allows downloads, the track page has a download button. Some downloads ask you to follow the artist or share the track first.

## Where the money goes

On a €5 monthly fan subscription, Stripe keeps about €0.45 (2.9% + €0.30), Tahti keeps €0.10 (a 2% operational fee), and the artist receives about €4.45. The operational fee pays for payment handling, billing support and data protection work; what's left of it goes into the yearly artist grant pool.

Downloads and fan support also count toward the artist's share of the yearly grants. See [Membership and governance](../account/membership-and-governance.md).
