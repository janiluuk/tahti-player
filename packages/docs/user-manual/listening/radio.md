# Radio

## Tahti Radio

Tahti Radio is the community's 24/7 stream. When nobody has booked the slot, it plays a fair rotation of tracks from Tahti artists; booked slots carry live shows.

Open **Radio** in the sidebar. Tahti Radio is one player at the top of the page:

- **Play Radio** tunes in. The player shows what's playing now, with the visualizer. **Open channel** opens the Tahti Radio channel page.
- Tabs under the player list the programme around now:
  - **Up next:** the next tracks in the rotation.
  - **Shows:** booked shows. **Full schedule** opens the programme schedule (`/schedule`), and signed-in artists can **Book a slot** to broadcast on Tahti Radio. Booked shows appear in the schedule and on Listen.
  - **Just played:** recent tracks, which you can play, queue or open for track info.
  - **Featured:** member channels recently relayed on Tahti Radio, each linking to its channel.

## Internet radio

Listen and Radio also list internet radio stations (YleX, Radio Helsinki, Radio Rock, Suomipop, Radio Nova and others). A station card plays the stream; its title opens the station page with what's on, when the station publishes it. The heart on a card adds the station to your favorites.

On a station page, the play button sits on the station's cover. To move to the next or previous station, swipe left or right, press **←** or **→** while the stage has focus, or use the small arrow buttons in its corner. Radio channels change station the same way.

Below Tahti Radio, the **Radio** page shows:

- **Favorite stations**, once you have some.
- The **Radio** row: the curated stations, the ones you turned on, and your own.
- **Add stations**: **Browse stations** opens the radio directory in Add-ons; **Add your own** takes a stream URL.

You can also add your own stations in **Settings → Playback**, and choose which stations show on Listen from the widget store (**+** on Listen).

{% hint style="info" %}
Some stations block playback from other websites. If a station won't start, the player says so; try again later or open the station's own site.
{% endhint %}
