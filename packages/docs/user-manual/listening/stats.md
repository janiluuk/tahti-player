# Listening stats

The **Stats** tab in **Listen → History** starts, when you're signed in, with **Across your devices**: time listened and number of listens for the last 7 days, 30 days or all time, and your top artists and tracks by time, counted on every device where you're signed in.

Below it, the stats for this device turn your history into:

- **Top artists**, **Top tracks** and **Top channels** for the range you pick (the last 30 days to start).
- **Time of day** and **Day of week**: when you listen.
- **Listening calendar**: a year of days, darker on days you listened more. On a narrow screen it opens on the latest weeks.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/listening-stats.png" alt="Listening stats with top lists and charts"><figcaption><p>Stats — tops, time of day, and calendar</p></figcaption></figure>

If nothing was played in the chosen range, it says so instead of showing empty charts.
